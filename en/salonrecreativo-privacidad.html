Privacy policy

Salón Recreativo
Last updated · July 2026

This application collects no personal data.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

  • It never connects to the internet.
  • It uses no analytics or measurement tools.
  • It shows no advertising.
  • It uses no tracking identifiers or cookies.
  • It requests no system permissions: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no notifications.
  • There are no accounts, no registration and no sign-in.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

WHAT IS STORED

Only what the machine needs to remember your games:

  • The scores on the high-score tables.
  • The three letters you enter when you make a table.
  • Whether you prefer sound on or off.

All of it is stored on your device only, in the app's own storage. It never leaves, it is never sent to any server and nobody else can see it — not even me. Uninstalling the app deletes that data with it.

CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data, so it collects no data from children either.

THIRD PARTIES

No information is shared with third parties, because there is no information to share. The app includes no advertising, analytics or social-media libraries.

CHANGES

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before it is released.
